OperatorOS vs. what we're building in-house

OperatorOS (operatoros.ai) is an AI automation layer for service franchises: agents for sales, recruiting, marketing, finance and comms, plugged into a CRM like ServiceTitan. That is, structurally, the same thesis as HA OS + our AI agents. This page frames the decision the way we agreed on July 8: cost, coverage, ROI.

Recommendation (working)

Keep building the core, use them as a benchmark — and validate our thesis with their demo. OperatorOS is proof there's a market for exactly what we're building internally. Buying it would solve visibility faster but puts our operational data and workflows on someone else's platform, priced per location as we scale. The strongest play: take their demo/quote, use it as the price ceiling our internal build must beat, and treat feature gaps they reveal as our roadmap input.

Buy vs build — the honest trade

What buying gets us

  • Speed: working agents in weeks, not months
  • Their learning curve — patterns proven on other franchises
  • A recruiting module we don't have at all
  • Forces discipline: clean CRM data, defined workflows

Why owning it is a moat

  • Our operational data stays ours — the compounding asset
  • Custom to multi-brand HA structure, not franchise templates
  • No per-location tax as we scale locations and brands
  • Productizable: OperatorOS proves people pay for this — a venture-studio play, not just an internal tool
  • No vendor lock-in on core operations
