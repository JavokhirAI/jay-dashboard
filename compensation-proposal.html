Why the current model doesn't work

  • The model works only when a task depends on me alone — and it proved it. In month 3 of the trial I had a concrete task I fully owned, the KPI worked, and it paid $2,000. That month I earned $4,000 total.
  • Since then, the KPI depends on factors outside my control. My role shifted to cross-team coordination and management — there is no longer a project that depends only on me. The savings-based bonus is tied to decisions, approvals and timelines owned by other teams, and for two months in a row it has paid $0 regardless of my own work.
  • In practice, variable pay has become no pay. The real compensation is simply the $2,000 floor — for a role that has grown far beyond what that floor was set for.
  • The base was priced for a different job. $2,000 was set for a coordinator. What the company actually gets is someone running IT operations, managing people, and working directly with core — business, finance, marketing and management, not ticket execution.
